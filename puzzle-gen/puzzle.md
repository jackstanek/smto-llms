The following is a logic puzzle about workplace relations within a company. The company has some number of departments and is staffed by employees, including a CEO, department heads, and other staff. The facts below describe who manages whom, who works in which department, and which firing or expense-approval actions have occurred. The rules describe how authority is derived from these facts. Use the facts and rules to answer the question at the end.

=====

We know the following facts:

- the head of Operations manages Josiah

- the head of Engineering manages Nelson
- the head of Sales manages Sean

- Roger manages Hailey

- Sean manages Landon
- Deanna works in Engineering
- Nelson works in Engineering
- Bridget works in Engineering
- Seth works in Engineering
- Fernando works in Sales
- Hailey works in Sales
- the head of Engineering works in Engineering
- Sean works in Sales

- Jerry works in Operations
- Gabriella works in Operations
- Hector works in Sales
- Tonya works in Operations


Rules
=====

- Anyone up the chain of command can approve expenses for someone below them
- Anyone up the chain of command can fire someone below them
- If p approved q's expense then p had authority to do so
- If p fired q then p had authority to do so
- No one can fire themselves
- No one manages themselves
- The management relation is anti-symmetric
- Managers can fire their direct reports
- No one can approve their own expenses


Question
========

Is it the case that the CEO can approve an expense for Seth?
